# Trademark Policy

"[Project Name]" and the [Project Name] logo are trademarks of [Trademark
Owner — e.g. the project maintainers, or a foundation]. This policy
explains how you can and can't use them.

## What this policy covers

The project's name, logo, and any other brand assets. It does **not**
cover the source code itself, which is licensed separately under the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) — an open source license grants rights to the code, not
to the trademarks.

## Uses that are always fine

- Referring to the project by name in blog posts, talks, articles, or
  documentation, to accurately describe compatibility or usage
- Saying your product "works with" or "is built on" [Project Name]
- Linking to the project's website or repository

## Uses that need permission

- Using the name or logo in your own product, company, or domain name in
  a way that could imply official endorsement
- Modifying the logo
- Selling merchandise featuring the name or logo

To request permission, contact **[INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## Why this policy exists

Trademark policies protect users from confusion about what's official and
what isn't — they're about brand identity, not about restricting use of
the code, which remains fully open source under its license.
